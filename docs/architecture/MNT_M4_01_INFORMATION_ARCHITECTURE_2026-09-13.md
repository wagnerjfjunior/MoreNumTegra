# MNT-M4-01 — Information Architecture

Status: `COMPLETE_CANDIDATE / AUTHORIZED`

Execution base: merged MNT-M3-07 main SHA `09405ae2c002b7e3b3298ca3a4fb434338b0ad3e`.

## Purpose
Translate accepted M3 ownership into governed information architecture.

## Precedence
Exact project > master development > stage or verified location > portfolio root.

## Core rules
- Caminhos da Lapa owns master-development intent; child projects keep exact-project ownership.
- Price, address, metragem, planta and availability remain inside the resolved exact-project owner.
- Stage surfaces own stage discovery only.
- Location routes require the verified-project-set fact gate.
- Support-only and excluded families do not receive doorway pages.
- Historical or sold surfaces must not imply active inventory.
- Route reservation prevents collision but does not prove demand or indexability.
- M3-04 release-time revalidation remains authoritative for volatile claims.

## Surface classes
Portfolio root; Caminhos da Lapa master; exact-project namespace; launch/construction/ready stage surfaces; verified-location pattern; project sections; support-only and excluded no-route states.

Machine-readable registry: `docs/architecture/data/MNT_M4_01_IA_SURFACE_REGISTRY.csv`.

## Boundary
M4-01 does not implement routes, content, schema, internal links, canonical, redirects, sitemap, Green, Vercel, Search Console, Ads or spend.
